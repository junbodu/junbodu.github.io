Hi! My name is Junbo (Timothy) Du and I am currently a first year Computer Science PhD student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) advised by Professor [Yongjoo Park](https://yongjoopark.com/). My research interests surround **scalable data systems for the age of unstructured and AI-driven workloads**, including vector databases, semantic search, distributed query processing, and LLM agents. I was previously a member of the University of Toronto [Far Data Lab](https://fardatalab.org/) where I was advised by Professor [Qizhen Zhang](https://qizhenzhang.me/). I also spent 8 months at [DRDC](https://www.canada.ca/en/defence-research-development.html) as a robotics research assistant, where I worked with Professor [Francois Grondin](https://francoisgrondin.com/en/index.html) on [Sound Source Localization for Human-Robot Interaction in Outdoor Environments](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11247033), published at IROS 2025.
