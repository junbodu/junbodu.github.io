Hi! My name is Junbo (Timothy) Du and I am currently a first year Computer Science PhD student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) advised by Professor [https://yongjoopark.com/](Yongjoo Park). My research interests lie in the intersection of agentic systems and database, with an focus on information retrieval and semantic operators. I was previously a member of the University of Toronto [https://fardatalab.org/](Far Data Lab) where I was advised by Professor [https://qizhenzhang.me/](Qizhen Zhang). I also spent 8 months at [https://www.canada.ca/en/defence-research-development.html](DRDC) as a robotics research assistant, where I worked with Professor [https://francoisgrondin.com/en/index.html](Francois Grondin) on [https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11247033](Sound Source Localization for Human-Robot Interaction in Outdoor Environments), published at IROS 2025.
